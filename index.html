Protocol Snapshot

MAYDAY

Use for grave and imminent danger requiring immediate assistance.

PAN PAN

Use for urgent situations affecting a vessel or person without immediate grave danger.

SECURITE

Use for navigational or meteorological safety information.

  • Distress traffic has absolute priority.
  • On hearing an unanswered Mayday, respond if you can assist and relay if needed.
  • Urgency and safety traffic normally move to a working channel after the announcement.
  • Copy position, nature of hazard, assistance needed, and persons on board.

Design Note

This prototype is inspired by GMDSS: A User's Handbook (6th edition) and built from current official procedure guidance.

Use it as a revision trainer, not as a substitute for your course notes, approved phraseology sheets, or instructor guidance.
